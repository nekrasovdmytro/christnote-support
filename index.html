About ChristNote

ChristNote is a Bible study and Christian note-taking app designed specifically for believers who want to deepen their faith through organized, private note-taking. Whether you're taking sermon notes, journaling your devotions, studying Scripture, or reflecting on your spiritual journey, ChristNote provides powerful tools while keeping your personal reflections completely private and secure.

Built with privacy-first principles, all your Bible study notes, prayer journals, sermon notes, and spiritual reflections are stored locally on your device. No cloud sync means your personal faith journey remains completely private and secure.

Key Features for Bible Study & Christian Note-Taking

📖 Bible Study Notes

Take detailed notes during sermons, Bible studies, and personal devotions. Organize your thoughts on Scripture passages, sermon points, and spiritual insights.

📝 Rich Text Editor

Format your notes with headers, lists, highlights, and more. Perfect for organizing sermon outlines, Bible study notes, and prayer journal entries.

🙏 Prayer Journal

Keep a private prayer journal with voice notes, reminders for prayer requests, and organized entries. Your prayers stay completely private on your device.

🔔 Reminders

Set reminders for daily devotions, prayer times, or to review specific notes. Notifications deep-link directly to your notes.

🎤 Voice Notes

Record sermon audio, prayer requests, or quick thoughts. Perfect for capturing insights during church services or personal reflection time.

📎 Attachments

Attach sermon PDFs, Bible study handouts, or images to your notes. Keep all your study materials organized in one place.

🔒 Complete Privacy

All your Bible study notes, prayer journals, and spiritual reflections are stored locally on your device. No cloud sync means your faith journey stays completely private.

📤 Export & Share

Export your notes with optional encryption. Share sermon notes with your small group or create PDFs of your Bible study insights.

🏷️ Organization

Use tags, projects, and templates to organize notes by book of the Bible, sermon series, prayer topics, or any system that works for you.

🤖 AI Assistance

Optional AI help for summarizing sermon notes, improving your writing, or translating content. Your data never leaves your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

▼
What is ChristNote?
ChristNote is a privacy-first notes app for iOS with rich text editing, reminders, voice notes, attachments, and optional AI assistance. Notes are stored locally on-device by default.
What iOS version is required?
iOS 15.0 or later is required. The app supports iPhone 13 and newer, including iPhone 17.
How do I create my first note?
Tap the floating "+" button (if enabled) or tap the "Local Notes" tab and use the create button. You can also use the template picker to create notes from templates.
How do I navigate between different sections?
Use the bottom menu bar to switch between tabs. You can hide the menu by tapping the chevron down button, and show it again by tapping the icon in the bottom-right corner.
Can I customize which tabs appear in the menu?
Yes, go to Settings → Dashboards to enable or disable specific dashboard tabs. You can also reorder tabs in Settings → Appearance → Tab Order.

Core Notes Features

▼
How do I create a new note?
• Tap the floating "+" button (if enabled)
• Tap "Create Note" from the template picker
• Use the app icon shortcut "New Note"
• Swipe down on the notes list and tap "Create Note"
How do I edit a note?
Tap any note in the list to open it. The editor opens automatically and you can start typing.
How do I delete a note?
• Swipe left on a note in the list and tap "Delete"
• Open a note, tap the menu (⋮) button, and select "Delete"
• Use multi-select mode: tap the selection icon, select notes, then tap delete
Can I recover deleted notes?
Yes! Go to Settings → Data Management → Deleted Notes to view and restore deleted notes. Notes are kept for 30 days before permanent deletion.
How do I pin a note to the top?
• Swipe right on a note in the list and tap "Pin"
• Open a note, tap the menu (⋮) button, and select "Pin"
• Use multi-select mode to pin multiple notes at once
How do I search for notes?
Use the search bar at the top of the notes list. You can search by note content, tags, or note names.
Can I sort my notes?
Yes, go to Settings → Notes → Sort Order. Options include:
• Most Recent (default)
• Oldest First
• Alphabetical
What are note names?
Notes can have optional names/titles. If not set, the app extracts the first line or header as a preview. You can set a custom name by tapping the note name field at the top of the editor.
How do I rename a note?
Open the note and tap on the name field at the top. You can also use the menu (⋮) → "Rename Note".
What are projects?
Projects help organize notes into groups. You can assign notes to projects and filter by project in the notes list.
How do I assign a note to a project?
Open a note, tap the menu (⋮) button, and select "Assign to Project". You can also use multi-select to assign multiple notes.
Can I filter notes by project?
Yes, use the filter bar in the notes list to select a project filter.
What is multi-select mode?
Tap the selection icon (checkmark) in the notes list toolbar to enter multi-select mode. You can then select multiple notes and perform bulk actions like delete, pin, tag, or assign to project.
How do I duplicate a note?
Open a note, tap the menu (⋮) button, and select "Duplicate Note".
Can I share notes with other apps?
Yes, open a note, tap the menu (⋮) button, and select "Share". You can export as PDF, RTF (Word-compatible), Markdown, or plain text.
What are connected notes?
Connected notes allow you to link related notes together. This is useful for organizing related content, creating knowledge graphs, or building book chapters.
How do I connect notes?
Open a note, tap the menu (⋮) button, and select "Connected Notes". Search and select notes to connect. Tap again to disconnect.
Can I see all notes connected to a specific note?
Yes, connected notes appear at the bottom of the note editor. Tap on a connected note to open it.
What are prophetic notes?
Prophetic notes are special note types for dreams, visions, and inspirations. They can be organized by type and viewed in a calendar format.
How do I create a prophetic note?
Use the "dream" template or add the "prophetic" tag. You can also filter by prophetic type in the notes list.
What is the Prophetic Calendar?
The Prophetic Calendar shows prophetic notes organized by date. Access it from the notes list or via app shortcuts.
Can I filter notes by prophetic type?
Yes, use the prophetic type filter in the notes list to filter by Dream, Vision, or Inspiration.

Rich Text Editor

▼
What editor engines are available?
The app supports two editor engines:
• iOS Native Editor (default) - Uses UITextView for native iOS editing
• HTML Editor - Alternative editor for web-based content

You can switch between them in Settings → Editor → Editor Engine.
What formatting options are available?
The editor supports:
• Headers (H1, H2, H3)
• Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough
• Text highlighting (7 colors)
• Text alignment (left, center, right, justify)
• Bulleted lists
• Numbered lists
• Dividers
• Clear formatting
How do I format text?
Select text and use the formatting toolbar that appears. You can also use keyboard shortcuts:
• **Bold:** ⌘B
• **Italic:** ⌘I
• **Underline:** ⌘U
What is Focus Mode?
Focus Mode provides a distraction-free writing experience by hiding the header and other UI elements. It's enabled by default but can be toggled in Settings → Editor.
How do I exit Focus Mode?
Tap the "Exit Focus Mode" button that appears, or swipe down from the top.
Can I customize the font?
Yes, go to Settings → Editor → Font Family to choose from bundled Google fonts or system fonts.
Can I adjust line spacing?
Yes, go to Settings → Editor → Line Spacing to adjust the spacing between lines.
How do I insert images?
• Tap the image button in the formatting toolbar
• Use Photos picker to select images
• Paste images directly from clipboard
• Use the camera button to take photos
Can I resize images in notes?
Yes, tap an image to select it, then use the resize options:
• Small (160px)
• Medium (240px)
• Large (320px)
• Fit to width
Can I draw on images?
Yes, select an image and tap "Draw on Image" to open the drawing editor.
How do I insert a divider?
Tap the divider button in the formatting toolbar, or type "---" and it will convert to a divider.
What are highlights?
Highlights allow you to mark important text with colored backgrounds. There are 7 highlight colors available.
How do I highlight text?
Select text and tap the highlight button in the formatting toolbar. Choose a color from the picker.
Can I remove highlighting?
Yes, select highlighted text and tap "Clear Highlight" or choose "None" from the highlight color picker.
How do I create lists?
Tap the list button in the formatting toolbar and choose "Bulleted List" or "Numbered List". You can also start typing "- " or "1. " to auto-create lists.
Can I nest lists?
Yes, indent list items using the indent buttons or by tapping Tab on the keyboard.
How do I clear all formatting?
Select text and tap "Clear Formatting" in the formatting toolbar.
Does the editor support Markdown?
The editor doesn't use Markdown syntax during editing, but you can export notes as Markdown. Some Markdown features are supported through the formatting toolbar.
Can I undo/redo changes?
Yes, shake your device or use the undo/redo buttons if available. Standard iOS text editing undo/redo works.
How do I copy formatted text?
Select text and use the standard copy gesture or menu. Formatted text is preserved when pasting within the app.
Can I paste plain text only?
Yes, when pasting, you'll see paste options. Choose "Paste as Plain Text" to remove formatting.

Privacy & Security

▼
Where are my notes stored?
Notes are stored locally on your device by default. You can optionally enable iCloud sync.
Are my notes encrypted?
Regular notes are stored in plain text locally. Secret notes are encrypted with a password you set.
Can I encrypt all my notes?
You can encrypt exports, but regular notes are stored unencrypted for performance. Use secret notes for sensitive content.
Does the app collect data?
No, the app is privacy-first and doesn't collect user data. All data stays on your device.
What about AI features?
When using AI features, your content is sent to the AI provider you configure. Review their privacy policy. AI is completely optional.
Are API keys secure?
Yes, API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain, which is encrypted and secure.
Can I use the app offline?
Yes, the app works fully offline. Only AI features require internet connectivity.
What permissions does the app need?
The app may request:
• Photo Library (to insert images)
• Camera (to take photos)
• Notifications (for reminders)
• Microphone (for voice notes)

All permissions are optional and only requested when needed.

Troubleshooting

▼
My notes aren't syncing to iCloud
• Check that iCloud is enabled in Settings → Storage & Sync
• Ensure you're signed into iCloud on your device
• Check iCloud storage space
• Try toggling iCloud sync off and on
The app is slow or laggy
• Try closing and reopening the app
• Check if you have many notes (consider archiving old ones)
• Disable unused dashboards
• Check available device storage
I can't find a note I created
• Check if filters are applied (tags, colors, search)
• Check if the note was deleted (go to Deleted Notes)
• Try searching with different keywords
• Check if the note is in a different project
Voice recordings aren't playing
• Check device volume
• Ensure the recording file exists (check Audio Dashboard)
• Try restarting the app
• Check if the note wasn't deleted
Images aren't loading
• Check photo library permissions
• Ensure images weren't deleted from Photos
• Try re-inserting the image
• Check available device storage
Reminders aren't working
• Check notification permissions in iOS Settings
• Ensure reminders are enabled in Settings → Reminders
• Check that notification time hasn't passed
• Verify device date/time is correct
AI features aren't working
• Check that AI is configured in Settings → AI Interactions
• Verify your API key is correct
• Check internet connectivity
• Review API usage limits with your provider
I forgot my secret note password
Unfortunately, secret notes are encrypted and cannot be recovered without the password. Make sure to remember or securely store your password.
The app crashed
• Try restarting the app
• Check for app updates
• If persistent, try resetting settings (this won't delete notes)
• Contact support with crash details
Can't export notes
• Check available device storage
• Ensure you have permission to save files
• Try exporting to a different location
• Check if the export file format is supported
Import isn't working
• Verify the file format is correct (JSON or CSV)
• Check if the file is encrypted (enter password if needed)
• Ensure the file isn't corrupted
• Check available device storage
Templates aren't showing
• Go to Settings → Templates to verify templates exist
• Try creating a new template
• Check if templates were accidentally deleted
Tags aren't appearing
• Check Settings → Tags to see if tags exist
• Try creating a new tag
• Verify filters aren't hiding tagged notes
