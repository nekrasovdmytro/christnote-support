About ChristNote

ChristNote is a Bible study and Christian note-taking app designed specifically for believers who want to deepen their faith through organized, private note-taking. Whether you're taking sermon notes, journaling your devotions, studying Scripture, or reflecting on your spiritual journey, ChristNote provides powerful tools while keeping your personal reflections completely private and secure.

Built with privacy-first principles, all your Bible study notes, prayer journals, sermon notes, and spiritual reflections are stored locally on your device. No cloud sync means your personal faith journey remains completely private and secure.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new note?
Tap the "+" button in the main notes list to create a new note. Perfect for taking sermon notes, Bible study notes, or journaling your devotions. You can also use the widget on your home screen for quick note creation, or use templates for consistent formatting.
Where are my Bible study notes and prayer journals stored?
All your notes - including sermon notes, Bible study notes, prayer journals, and spiritual reflections - are stored locally on your device. They never leave your device and are not synced to any external servers. This ensures your personal faith journey remains completely private and secure. You have complete control over your data.
Can I export my notes?
Yes! You can export your notes from the Settings menu. You can choose to export with or without encryption, and save them wherever you prefer. You can also export individual notes as PDF or Word-compatible RTF files.
How do I use voice notes for sermons or prayers?
When editing a note, tap the microphone icon to record a voice note. Perfect for capturing sermon audio, recording prayer requests, or quickly capturing thoughts during Bible study. You can play, rename, and manage all your recordings from Settings. Recordings can also be exported.
What is the Web Notes feature?
Web Notes allows you to connect to external web-based note-taking services. You provide the URL, and the app displays the web service in a browser view. We don't access or store content from external services.
Does ChristNote collect my data?
No. ChristNote is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All your notes remain on your device under your complete control.
How do I set up reminders for devotions or prayer times?
From the notes list, long-press on a note and select "Add Reminder" from the context menu. Perfect for setting daily devotion reminders, prayer time alerts, or reminders to review specific Bible study notes. You can set the date and time, and the notification will deep-link directly to your note when it fires.
Can I use templates for sermon notes or Bible study?
Yes! Templates allow you to create reusable note formats perfect for consistent sermon note-taking, Bible study outlines, or prayer journal entries. You can create and manage templates from the Settings menu, and use them when creating new notes. Templates support localization for multiple languages.
How do I organize my Bible study notes with tags?
When creating or editing a note, use the tag selector to add tags. Perfect for organizing by book of the Bible, sermon series, prayer topics, or study themes. You can then filter and search notes by tags. Tags help you organize and find your notes quickly.
How do I set up AI assistance?
AI features are optional. Go to Settings and configure your AI provider (OpenAI, Claude, or DeepSeek). You'll need to provide an API key, which is securely stored in your device's Keychain. Once configured, you can use "Ask AI" actions in the editor.

Getting Started with Bible Study & Note-Taking

New to ChristNote? Here are some quick tips to get started with your Bible study and Christian note-taking:

  • Create your first note: Tap the "+" button to start taking sermon notes, Bible study notes, or begin your prayer journal
  • Format your notes: Select text to see formatting options (bold, italic, headers, etc.) - perfect for organizing sermon outlines
  • Add images: Tap the image icon while editing to insert photos of sermon slides or Bible study handouts
  • Set devotion reminders: Long-press a note and select "Add Reminder" to set daily devotion or prayer time alerts
  • Organize with tags: Add tags like "Sermon Notes", "Prayer Requests", or book names (e.g., "John", "Romans") when creating or editing notes
  • Use templates: Create templates in Settings for consistent sermon note formats or Bible study outlines
  • Record voice notes: Use the microphone icon to record sermon audio or quick prayer thoughts
  • Export your notes: Go to Settings to export notes, photos, and recordings - share sermon notes with your small group or create PDFs
